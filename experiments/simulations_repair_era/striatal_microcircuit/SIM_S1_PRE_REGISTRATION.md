## 1. Domain Hypothesis & Theoretical Motivation

`gtt.tex` formally disclaims microscopic non-classical phenomena, situating the Master Law $\Delta S_t = \lambda(V_t - E_t)$ as a macroscopic, mean-field description of trust. However, for biological fidelity, the macroscopic delta rule must mathematically emerge from microscopic synaptic plasticity. In the striatum, Medium Spiny Neurons (MSNs) integrate cortical inputs (expectations) and dopaminergic signals (Reward Prediction Errors, RPE) to update synaptic weights via an eligibility trace mechanism.
**Hypothesis (S1):** A continuous-time biophysical model of MSN eligibility traces (where presynaptic activity leaves a decaying calcium/NMDA trace $c(t)$, which is subsequently multiplied by a phasic dopamine burst $D(t) \propto V-E$) will produce a terminal synaptic weight update $\Delta W$ that linearly correlates with the macroscopic discrete Master Law update $\Delta S_t$.

## 2. Frozen Quantitative Criteria

### Criterion C1 (Macroscopic Reduction):
The terminal synaptic weight change $\Delta W$ generated by integrating the microscopic continuous-time differential equations (eligibility trace $dc/dt$ and dopaminergic burst $D(t)$) must yield a Pearson correlation $r \ge 0.95$ with the ideal macroscopic discrete update $\lambda (V - E)$ across 1,000 randomized $(V, E)$ event pairs.

## 3. Experimental Parameters & Methodology
- $N = 1,000$ simulated events. $E \in [0.1, 0.9]$, $V \in [0.1, 0.9]$.
- **Microscopic Model (Euler Integration, $dt=1\text{ms}$, 1000ms window):**
  - Presynaptic expectation pulse at $t=200\text{ms}$, amplitude proportional to $E$.
  - Postsynaptic validation pulse at $t=300\text{ms}$, amplitude proportional to $V$.
  - Eligibility trace $c(t)$ decays with $\tau_c = 100\text{ms}$.
  - Dopamine release $D(t)$ peaks at $t=300\text{ms}$, proportional to $V - E$.
  - $\Delta W = \eta \int c(t) D(t) dt$.
- **Random Seed:** Strict `seed=42`.
