    *   **Signed Mismatch ($\varepsilon_t$):** Every technical paper enforces the exact same stack-wide convention: $\varepsilon_t := V_t - E_t$ (validation minus expectation, where positive $\varepsilon_t$ denotes reality confirming or exceeding expectation). 
    *   **Normalized Mismatch ($\tilde{\varepsilon}$):** Explicitly defined in the Whitepaper (Eq. 1) as $\tilde{\varepsilon} = \frac{V - E}{\max(s_d, \epsilon_{\min})}$ with per-domain scale $s_d$ (RMSE of the expectation distribution) and regularization floor $\epsilon_{\min} = 10^{-4}$ to guarantee numerical stability against zero-variance history profiles.
    *   **Stabilization Update ($\Delta S_t$):** Consistently stated across the entire stack as the Rescorla–Wagner delta rule applied to trust: $\Delta S_t = \lambda(V_t - E_t) = \lambda\varepsilon_t$, with recognition-stage gating denoted via $r_t$ in GTT (§2.2).

### 2. Cross-Document Narrative Arc (Philosophy $\to$ Physics $\to$ Biology $\to$ Protocol $\to$ Application)

The architectural progression across the six documents is seamless, operating as a tightly locked deductive and engineering stack where each layer solves a constraint left open by the layer above it:

1.  **Philosophical & Ontological Anchor (`the_value_of_trust.md` & `collection.md`):** Establishes the foundational premise that trust is not a voluntary moral sentiment or cortical invention, but an involuntary, perception-like biophysical binding dynamic ("the brain did not invent trust; trust invented the brain") that precedes neurons by over a billion years (citing cellular gap-junction bioelectric stress-sharing vs. unicellular defection/cancer) and scales through infant attachment (Erikson, Bowlby/Ainsworth) to economic credit. Crucially, the author quarantines personal moral conviction (the ladder from *self-love $\to$ choices $\to$ discipline $\to$ self-respect $\to$ credibility*) in the preface so that no downstream mathematical or engineering claim relies on unformalized philosophy.
2.  **Physics & Information Geometry (`gtt_revised.md`):** Translates the philosophical definition into a scale-invariant dynamical system ($T = [D,O,C,A,S]$, $\Delta S_t = \lambda(V_t - E_t)$). It supplies the Fisher–Rao metric on the stage-activity simplex, Lyapunov stability of the $E=V$ alignment line, Langevin/Fokker–Planck stochastic extensions, observation-distortion mechanics ($\psi$), and a Kuramoto-type collective phase transition (`[ANALOGY]`). GTT intentionally leaves open *how* a biological brain computes this vector and *how* an institution can record it without triggering $\psi$-distortion.
3.  **Neurobiological Instantiation (`docas_revised.md`):** Supplies the biological coordinate system for GTT. Confronting the physiological reality that the five neuromodulators span $>4$ orders of magnitude in timescale ($0.1\text{ s}$ phasic dopamine/LC-NE bursts up to $\sim 2,700\text{ s}$ HPA-axis cortisol awakening responses; 5 orders including serotonergic tone), DOCAS explicitly rejects a serial five-step cascade in favor of a **parallel, multi-timescale modulation architecture** where "stage" means *functional role*. It bridges predictive processing ($\tau = -\mathrm{EFE}$) with constructed-emotion theory (Barrett) and sets up the dual-layer wearable + financial experimental protocol.
4.  **Decentralized Measurement Infrastructure (`tfp_revised.md`):** Translates GTT's observation-distortion parameter ($\psi$) and corrective bandwidth ($\mu$) into systems engineering constraints. Because centralized custodial databases minimize $\mu$ (stale feedback) and maximize negative $\psi$ (surveillance chilling effects and mega-breaches), TFP specifies a 5-layer user-owned edge architecture: W3C DIDs + SD-JWT (RFC 9901), database-per-tenant edge storage (libSQL), Substrate settlement/audit (Kusama "Spammening" benchmark), Smoldot light-client verification, and x402 micropayments. TFP strictly strips out all domain-specific and financial logic.
5.  **Financial Application (`whitepaper_revised.md`):** Configures TFP's domain-agnostic estimator for consumer credit (FreshCredit: *Report $\to$ Score $\to$ Offer*). It justifies financial transactions as the highest signal-to-noise validation substrate at population scale (standardized currency units lock the baseline against metric distortion) and pairs permissioned cash-flow underwriting with an opt-in physiological wearable stream that is used strictly to validate DOCAS in silico/in vivo while being **structurally excluded from credit underwriting decisions**.

### 3. Discrepancies, Contradictions, and Epistemic Discipline Audit

A rigorous cross-check of theoretical claims against empirical assertions reveals **zero unacknowledged contradictions** across the 6-paper suite. In fact, the collection’s defining strength is its aggressive preemptive self-auditing. Where tensions exist between theory and empirical reality, the papers explicitly surface, quantify, and quarantine them:

*   **The Rousseau Vulnerability Boundary (Trust vs. Verification/Reputation):**
    *   *Potential Tension:* TFP processes low-stakes technical events (API calls, device attestations, credential checks), whereas GTT defines trust as requiring genuine vulnerability and interdependence (Rousseau et al.).
    *   *Resolution across papers:* TFP (§1.1) and the Whitepaper (§2.2) explicitly enforce the "Rousseau boundary." Vulnerability-free events are classified strictly as *verification/reputation measurements*. Moreover, the Whitepaper labels the bridge from contract-enforced credit repayment (assurance-channel compliance) to intrinsic trustworthiness as a `[HYPOTHESIS]` to be tested via the §3.4 concordance pilot, refusing to conflate compliance with trust.
*   **Candidate-Gene Failure (`PDE4B` rs7528604):**
    *   *Potential Tension:* Linking the mathematical parameter $\lambda$ to a specific genetic locus risks repeating the post-2019 candidate-gene replication crisis (OXTR, SLC6A4, COMT, DRD4).
    *   *Resolution across papers:* DOCAS (§4.2) and `collection.md` enforce a strict two-gate evidentiary standard (genome-wide significance + independent replication) and openly declare that their own lead candidate, `PDE4B` (rs7528604), **fails the second gate**—passing in iPSYCH ($p = 5.39 \times 10^{-11}$, $\text{OR} = 0.89$) but failing in the Purves et al. independent anxiety GWAS lookup ($p = .18$, $\text{OR} = 0.97$). The `PDE4B` $\to$ cAMP $\to \lambda$ pathway is strictly quarantined as `[HYPOTHESIS]`.
*   **In-Silico vs. In-Vivo Neurochemistry & Analytical Circularity:**
    *   *Potential Tension:* Claiming empirical validation from simulation or fMRI encoding models (TRIBE v2).
    *   *Resolution across papers:* DOCAS explicitly states in the Abstract that *no neurochemistry was measured anywhere in the paper*; TRIBE v2 forward encoding is labeled strictly as instrument-level pipeline validation, while actual carrier validation is gated behind the pre-registered human pharmacological/PET trial (`DOCAS-GATE-1`). Similarly, GTT notes that its in-silico post-betrayal ringing period of $7.15$ steps vs. the analytical root prediction of $7.17$ steps is merely an "implementation check, not corroboration."
*   **Surveillance Distortion & Per-Tenant Security Trade-offs:**
    *   *Potential Tension:* Overclaiming that decentralization eliminates surveillance bias or data breaches.
    *   *Resolution across papers:* Both TFP and the Whitepaper state two hard boundaries up front: (1) Empirical evidence for surveillance chilling effects (Penney, 2016: $\sim 19\%$ drop in privacy-sensitive Wikipedia traffic) is strong, whereas experimental demand-characteristics literature is heterogeneous with frequent nulls (12 of 19 positive, 5 clear nulls); further, user-owned measurement only reduces $\psi \to \psi_{\min} < 0$, never eliminating distortion. (2) Database-per-tenant custody converts rare-catastrophic organizational breaches into frequent-small endpoint losses—a variance reduction whose expected-loss win region is strictly conditional on $p_{\mathrm{device}} + p_{\mathrm{commonmode}} < p_{\mathrm{org}}$, backed by an explicit Number Needed to Harm (NNH) accounting in the Whitepaper.
*   **Primary-Source Statistical Hygiene:**
    *   Across TFP and the Whitepaper, industry myths and outdated figures are systematically corrected: the unverified "95% of synthetic identities pass onboarding" vendor blog stat is rejected; the 2024 ITRC breach victim notice count is cited at its restated, de-duplicated figure ($1.367\text{ billion}$, down from $1.73\text{ billion}$); Kusama's December 2024 "Spammening" throughput ($143,343\text{ batch TPS}$ on 23/100 cores) is paired with ordinary P2P transfer peaks ($10,920\text{ TPS}$); x402's $169\text{M}$ year-one payment volume is explicitly flagged as memecoin-inflated; and the CFPB's 2015 credit-invisibility numbers ($26\text{M}$ invisible / $19\text{M}$ unscorable) are updated throughout to the June 2025 CFPB technical correction ($7.0\text{M}$ invisible [2.7%] + $\sim 25.3\text{M}$ unscored thin/stale [9.8%] = $\sim 32\text{M}$ adults in the 2020 benchmark).
