### 1. The White Space: What the Trust Stack Captures That Current Literature Misses

Current literature across these four fields operates in isolated silos: neuroscientists model biological trust inside the skull; cryptographers model algorithmic trust across networks; and neuroeconomists model behavioral trust in game-theoretic markets. The Trust Stack’s primary innovation is the **vertical integration of biological predictive processing with cryptographic attestation**.

#### A. Bridging Internal Precision-Weighting with External Cryptographic Attestation
*   **The Literature Gap:** In Active Inference (Friston, Clark), an agent’s "trust" in a sensory signal is modeled mathematically as **precision-weighting** ($\Pi$)—the inverse variance of a prediction error. Meanwhile, in Decentralized Identity (W3C DID, ZKP), trust is binary and exogenous: a cryptographic proof is either mathematically valid or invalid. Active Inference lacks a mechanism for an agent to offload the metabolic cost of precision-weighting onto verifiable external cryptographic proofs; conversely, DID systems treat the human user as a static, rational key-holder, ignoring the dynamic, precision-weighted Bayesian updating occurring in the user's brain.
*   **The Trust Stack Capture:** By linking **GTT** with **DOCAS**, the framework treats a cryptographic attestation (e.g., an SD-JWT or Zero-Knowledge Proof) not merely as an access token, but as an **exogenous hyper-prior** that directly modulates the precision-weighting ($\Pi$) of an agent's internal generative model. **TFP** acts as the metabolic bridge, proving that cryptographic verification is mathematically equivalent to minimizing Variational Free Energy ($F$) across a multi-agent boundary at near-zero cognitive cost.

#### B. Solving the "Trusted Oracle" Problem in Neural Decoding
*   **The Literature Gap:** Neural decoding (Gallant, Beliy/Brain-IT) and connectomics (FlyWire, MICrONS) focus on the *readout* of latent neural representations and structural wiring. However, as Brain-Computer Interfaces (BCIs) scale, there is a massive gap regarding the **epistemic provenance of decoded neural states**. How does an external system verify that a reconstructed image or semantic vector genuinely originated from a specific biological connectome without exposing the raw, privacy-violating neural data?
*   **The Trust Stack Capture:** **DOCAS + TFP** provides a cryptographic wrapper for neural states. By utilizing zero-knowledge attestations over neural latent spaces, the Trust Stack allows an agent to prove properties about their internal cognitive state (e.g., "I am in a high-trust/low-volatility attentional state" or "This semantic reconstruction originated from my biological generative model") without revealing the underlying connectomic weights or raw fMRI/electrophysiological readout.

#### C. Extending Neuroeconomic Volatility to Network Topologies
*   **The Literature Gap:** Neuroeconomics (Behrens, Rescorla-Wagner) successfully models how single agents dynamically adjust their learning rates ($\alpha$) based on estimated environmental volatility ($v$) in social interactions. However, this literature breaks down in N-player decentralized networks where agents do not repeatedly interact with the same counterparty, rendering standard associative learning rules useless against Sybil attacks or algorithmic spoofing.
*   **The Trust Stack Capture:** **GTT** generalizes Behrens’ hierarchical volatility learning to decentralized graph topologies. By anchoring reputation and identity in **DOCAS**, agents do not need to learn counterparty volatility from scratch via costly trial-and-error (Rescorla-Wagner prediction errors); instead, they inherit pre-computed, cryptographically verifiable volatility priors via **TFP**.
