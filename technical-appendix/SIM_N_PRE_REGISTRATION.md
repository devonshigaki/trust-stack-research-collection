## 1. Ontological Framework & Theoretical Motivation

Trust is a fundamental physical force vector—the expectation-validation differential ($\varepsilon_t = V_t - E_t$)—present in all systems from ion channels to societal ledgers. However, in the *human species*, this force vector is processed through the cognitive architecture of consciousness as a **constructed perceptual phenomenon**, analogous to pitch or loudness. 

The human brain uses "unconscious inference" to build a perceptual model of trust. Central to this perception is the philosophical prior of the **Markov Blanket**—the statistical boundary that separates the "self-model" from the "external-world-model."

- **Machiavellian (Individualist) Priors:** Characterized by a highly rigid, impermeable Markov blanket. The self is strictly isolated. Precision on external betrayal prediction errors ($\varepsilon_t < 0$) is hyper-weighted ($\lambda^- \gg \lambda^+$). This optimizes for immediate threat detection but incurs massive allostatic/metabolic cost (Cortisol overload).
- **Non-Dual (Buddhist) Priors:** Characterized by the progressive dissolution of the Markov blanket. The perceptual distinction between self and external reality is erased. External states are processed as internal states, neutralizing the precision of adversarial prediction errors. Variational free energy and metabolic cost are minimized (equanimity).
