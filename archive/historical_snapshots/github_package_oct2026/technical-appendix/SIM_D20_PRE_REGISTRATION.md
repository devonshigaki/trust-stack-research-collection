## 1. Domain Hypotheses & Theoretical Motivation

Acoustic time-compression ("fast-talking") is a widespread cloaking tactic in financial and pharmaceutical marketing. When syllable rates exceed the theta-band phase-locking limit of the auditory cortex ($r_{\text{crit}} \approx 9.5\text{--}10.5\text{ syl/s}$), bottom-up phonetic evidence collapses into un-parseable noise. Under the **Phonemic Restoration Effect (Warren, 1970)**, the brain actively replaces this missing or noisy evidence with top-down optimistic expectations ($V_{\text{promo}}$), preventing the threat from breaching the reality threshold ($m \le T_{\text{reality}}$).
